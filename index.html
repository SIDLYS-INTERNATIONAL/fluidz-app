Three steps

  1. Get the link

    Enter your e-mail and open the link on your computer: the download appears on this page.

  2. Install

    Unzip the file. Open chrome://extensions (paste it in the address bar), switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

  3. Search

    Open web.grindr.com in grid view, apply your filters, then type a name or a word.

Questions

Does anything leave my browser?

No. The extension makes no network requests. Your searches and settings stay in your browser’s local storage, on your computer.

Does it access my Grindr account?

No. It never asks for your Grindr login and never reads your messages. It only looks at the names shown on the grid you have open.

Does it click for me?

Never: no taps, no favorites, no messages. It can scroll the grid to load more profiles. Keep the speed on Gentle, the default: a few people saw the Grindr Web chat disconnect after very long, fast scans. Grindr’s terms are strict about third-party tools, so use it at your own discretion.

How do I update it?

Unzip the new version over the same folder, open chrome://extensions and click the reload arrow ↻ on its card. Your saved searches are kept. Tick the box above to hear about new versions.
