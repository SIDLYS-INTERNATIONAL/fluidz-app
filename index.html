  • Keyboard shortcuts

    Alt+Shift+S start / pause, Alt+Shift+F search field, Alt+Shift+N / P next and previous match. Change them in chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

  • My data, backup, self-test

    See what is stored, export or import one backup file, erase everything. The grid self-test copies a diagnostic with no names.

Installation

Install it in 5 steps.

Chrome only accepts extensions from its store or in “developer mode”. Profile Name Search isn’t on the store (here’s why below), so you add it by hand — it takes 2 minutes.

You’re in. Next: install it.

  1. 1

    Get the link by e-mail, download and unzip

    Enter your e-mail at the top of this page. We send a one-time link: open it on your computer and the download appears right here. Chrome can’t read a .zip file, so unzip it first. On a Mac, double-click the .zip: a folder “profile-name-search” appears in Downloads. On Windows: right-click the .zip → Extract All → Extract.

    Get the link by e-mail

    Keep this folder where it is (Downloads or Documents): Chrome reads the extension from it every time. If you delete or move it, the extension stops.

  2. 2

    Open Chrome’s extensions page

    Copy this address, paste it into Chrome’s address bar and press Enter.

    chrome://extensions

    Why copy and not a link? For security, Chrome blocks websites from opening this page. Other way: menu ⋮ → Extensions → Manage extensions.

  3. 3

    Turn on Developer mode

    Top-right of the page, switch on “Developer mode”. Three new buttons appear on the left.

    Developer mode only lets you load extensions from a folder. You can switch it off later — but then the extension stops.

  4. 4

    Click “Load unpacked” and pick the folder

    Click “Load unpacked” (top-left). In the window that opens, select the unzipped folder — not the .zip — the one that directly contains manifest.json, then click Select.

    Got “Manifest file is missing”? You picked the wrong level: open the folder once more and select the one inside.

  5. 5

    Pin its icon and open Grindr Web

    Click the puzzle icon in Chrome’s toolbar, then the pin next to Profile Name Search: its pink-and-purple icon stays in your toolbar. Click it anytime to show, hide or reset the panel. Then open web.grindr.com — the panel appears with a short 4-step welcome.

Once it’s installed

How to use it well.

On your first visit to Grindr Web, a 4-step welcome walks you through it, starting with “Get your grid ready”. ⚙ → Replay the tour shows it again anytime.

  • Open Grindr Web, filter, then search

    Open web.grindr.com in grid view and apply your Grindr filters first (position, age, favorites, online…). The panel appears: type what you’re looking for and start.

  • Type names, keywords or emojis

    “jay”, “TOP”, “🚀”… Combine them: a comma (OR), a + (AND), -word to leave a word out, (a, b) for synonyms. See the examples below, or press ? in the panel.

  • Start auto-scroll and let it load

    It scrolls the grid for you and loads new profiles. Leave it running: the counters show how many profiles were scanned and found.

    Scan speed: Gentle (the default) takes short breaks to keep Grindr Web happy. Fast is the old pace and can disconnect Grindr Web’s chat.

  • Pause, check, resume

    Pause anytime (or let it pause on each match) to look at the profiles found, then press Resume to continue down the grid.

  • The end of the grid

    When it stops, you’ve seen every profile Grindr shows you: that’s the limit of your Grindr plan, not of the extension. For more profiles, upgrade your Grindr plan.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is it safe?

The download is a zip of about 110 KB: plain, readable JavaScript, nothing minified. The extension only asks Chrome for local storage, only runs on web.grindr.com, and makes no network requests — nothing leaves your browser. Open content.js in any text editor to check.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Chrome extensions only run in desktop browsers: Chrome, Brave, Edge or Arc, version 116 or later. On your phone, enter your e-mail and open the link on your computer.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It’s free, with no trial and no limit.

Why do you ask for my e-mail?

It’s how we send you the download link, and how we can tell you when a new version is out, since updates are manual for now. The link creates a free Fluidz account, with no password. You only get news if you tick the box, and we never sell or share your address.

Can I get banned from Grindr for using it?

No tool can promise that. Grindr’s terms are strict about third-party tools. Profile Name Search never acts for you — no favorites, no messages, no taps — it only reads the names already on your screen and scrolls the grid. But auto-scroll is a grey zone, so use it at your own discretion.

Why isn’t it on the Chrome Web Store?

Grindr’s terms are strict about third-party tools. Profile Name Search never acts on its own — it only searches, scrolls and shows you a match when you ask — but auto-scroll is enough of a grey zone to keep it off the store. So we ship it directly, with plain, readable code and no network calls. Use it at your own discretion.

Which browsers?

Google Chrome, and browsers built on it that accept Chrome extensions: Brave, Edge, Arc. Not Safari or Firefox.

How can I see more profiles?

Grindr decides how many profiles load, depending on your plan. When the scroll stops, you’ve seen every profile Grindr shows you: that’s the limit of your current Grindr plan, not of the extension. To load more profiles, upgrade your Grindr subscription. The extension can only read what Grindr shows.

How do I update it?

Export a backup first (⚙ → My data). Then unzip the new download over the same folder, open chrome://extensions and click the reload arrow ↻ on its card. Don’t click Remove: removing deletes its data. Your saved searches are kept. Tick the box in the form to hear about new versions.

I used version 2. Will I lose my saved searches?

No. Unzip 3.0 over the same folder and reload it: your saved searches, current search, tab, language and settings move over by themselves, and the panel says “Your searches were kept”. Keep the same folder: Chrome links the extension’s data to it.

What does the extension store, and where?

Only on your computer, in Chrome’s local storage: settings, saved searches and folders, your marks, and the scan journal (names, photo links and dates — never the photos), 20 scans for 30 days. ⚙ → My data shows it, exports it to one backup file and erases it.

Which languages?

English, Français, Español, Português, Italiano and Deutsch. You pick yours in the extension’s welcome and can change it anytime.

Troubleshooting

Stuck? It’s usually one of these.

I don’t see the panel on Grindr Web.

Reload the tab (⌘R / Ctrl+R). Check that the extension’s switch is blue in chrome://extensions and that you’re on web.grindr.com — not the phone app.

Chrome says “Manifest file is missing or unreadable”.

You selected the wrong folder. Pick the folder that directly contains manifest.json (often the one inside the first folder).

I can’t find “Load unpacked”.

Developer mode is off: turn on the switch top-right of chrome://extensions and the button appears top-left.

Chrome shows a warning about developer-mode extensions.

That’s normal for any extension not installed from the store. Click × to dismiss it; it doesn’t affect anything.

Auto-scroll doesn’t move.

Scroll the grid a little by hand first, then press Start again. Grindr sometimes loads slowly.

Grindr Web chat got disconnected after a search.

It can happen after a long, fast scan: Grindr Web may drop its chat connection while thousands of profiles load. We can’t reproduce it ourselves and we don’t control Grindr’s side, so we can’t say for sure what triggers it or promise it’s gone. To get your chat back, reload the Grindr tab (⌘R / Ctrl+R): you stay signed in. For long chats, turn the extension off in chrome://extensions (or use another Chrome profile), or use the Grindr phone app. Version 2.1.0 makes the scan gentler: Scan speed is on Gentle by default, with short breaks taken automatically, while Fast (the old pace) may cause it. Update by downloading the new .zip and replacing the old folder.

The panel says “Grindr stopped responding”.

Grindr crashed or emptied the grid during a scan, so the scan paused and kept its results in the journal. Reload the Grindr tab if needed, then press Resume. After a reload, an unfinished scan is offered again.

The scan stopped by itself.

It reached the scan time limit (30 minutes by default, set in ⚙). Press Resume to go on, or pick another limit. If the panel warns that Grindr Web is open in another tab, close the other tab: use one tab at a time.
