How it works

Three steps, about two minutes.

You’re in. Next: install it.

  1. 1

    Get the link by e-mail

    Enter your e-mail at the top of this page. We send a one-time link: open it on your computer and the download appears right here.

  2. 2

    Install it

    For now the extension is added by hand, in Chrome’s developer mode.

    1. Unzip the download. Keep that folder where it is: Chrome reads the extension from it.
    2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome. Chrome blocks links to it, so copy the address and paste it in the address bar.
    3. Switch on Developer mode, top right.
    4. Click Load unpacked, then pick the unzipped folder: the one that directly contains manifest.json.
  3. 3

    Open Grindr Web, filter, then search

    Open web.grindr.com in grid view and apply your Grindr filters first (position, age, favorites, online…). The panel appears: type what you’re looking for and start.

Already installed? Updating

  1. Unzip the new download over the same folder, replacing the files.
  2. Open chrome://extensions.
  3. Click the reload arrow ↻ on the extension’s card. Don’t click Remove: your saved searches stay.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Chrome extensions only run in desktop browsers: Chrome, Brave, Edge or Arc, version 116 or later. On your phone, enter your e-mail and open the link on your computer.

Why do you ask for my e-mail?

It’s how we send you the download link, and how we can tell you when a new version is out, since updates are manual for now. The link creates a free Fluidz account, with no password. You only get news if you tick the box, and we never sell or share your address.

Is it safe? Can I get banned?

The extension is about 41 KB of readable JavaScript, it only runs on web.grindr.com and it makes no network requests. For your Grindr account, no tool can promise anything: Grindr’s terms are strict about third-party tools. It never acts for you (no favorites, no messages, no taps), but auto-scroll is a grey zone, so use it at your own discretion.

How do I update it?

Unzip the new download over the same folder, open chrome://extensions and click the reload arrow on its card. Don’t remove it: your saved searches are kept. Tick the box in the form to hear about new versions.

Is it on the Chrome Web Store?

Not for now. You add it in developer mode with Load unpacked (steps above). Grindr’s terms are strict about third-party tools, so we ship it directly, with plain, readable code.

My Grindr Web chat disconnected after a search.

A few people report it after a very long, fast scan: Grindr Web may drop its chat connection while thousands of profiles load. We can’t reproduce it ourselves and we don’t control Grindr’s side, so we can’t say what triggers it or promise it’s gone. Keep the scan speed on Gentle, the default. To get your chat back, reload the Grindr tab: you stay signed in.

Which languages?

English, Français, Español, Português, Italiano and Deutsch. You pick yours in the extension’s welcome and can change it anytime.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It’s free, with no trial and no limit.
