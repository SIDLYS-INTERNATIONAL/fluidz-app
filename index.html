FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is it safe?

The download is a zip of about 110 KB: plain, readable JavaScript, nothing minified. The extension only asks Chrome for local storage, only runs on web.grindr.com, and makes no network requests — nothing leaves your browser. Open content.js in any text editor to check.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Chrome extensions only run in desktop browsers: Chrome, Brave, Edge or Arc, version 116 or later. On your phone, enter your e-mail and open the link on your computer.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It’s free, with no trial and no limit.

How do I use it well?

On your first visit to Grindr Web, a 4-step welcome walks you through it, starting with “Get your grid ready”. ⚙ → Replay the tour shows it again anytime.

  1. Open Grindr Web, filter, then search Open web.grindr.com in grid view and apply your Grindr filters first (position, age, favorites, online…). The panel appears: type what you’re looking for and start.
  2. Type names, keywords or emojis “TOP”, “DOM”, “🚀”… Combine them: a comma (OR), a + (AND), -word to leave a word out, (a, b) for synonyms. See the examples below, or press ? in the panel.
  3. Start auto-scroll and let it load It scrolls the grid for you and loads new profiles. Leave it running: the counters show how many profiles were scanned and found. Scan speed: Gentle (the default) takes short breaks to keep Grindr Web happy. Fast is the old pace and can disconnect Grindr Web’s chat.
  4. Pause, check, resume Pause anytime (or let it pause on each match) to look at the profiles found, then press Resume to continue down the grid.
  5. The end of the grid When it stops, you’ve seen every profile Grindr shows you: that’s the limit of your Grindr plan, not of the extension. For more profiles, upgrade your Grindr plan.

Three tabs above the results — All, Favorites and Not favorites. Pick “Not favorites” to skip the guys you already favorited: the list, the grid outline and the auto-scroll pauses all follow the tab.

Best option: open it in Chrome’s side panel (toolbar icon → “Open in Chrome’s side panel”). It sits next to the page and never covers the grid. Prefer it floating over the page? Drag it by its handle (top or bottom), shrink it to a bubble, or park it on the screen edge. Lost it? Toolbar icon → Reset panel position.

Search like on Google

It searches the name shown on each profile card — so names, keywords and emojis all work. Upper or lower case doesn’t matter.

  • dom topTags in namesMany guys put tags in their name: DOM TOP, vers, bear… Search them like a name.
  • 🚀An emoji🚀, 🌴, 😈… emojis in names work too.
  • top, xxlOR — commaEither one: profiles with TOP or XXL in their name, in one pass.
  • dom + topAND — plusBoth words, any order: “DOM TOP 🚀” and “top dom” match, “dom” alone doesn’t.
  • "dom top"Exact phraseOnly these words, in this order.

How do I update it?

Export a backup first (⚙ → My data). Then unzip the new download over the same folder, open chrome://extensions and click the reload arrow ↻ on its card. Don’t click Remove: removing deletes its data. Your saved searches are kept. Tick the box in the form to hear about new versions.

No. Unzip 3.0 over the same folder and reload it: your saved searches, current search, tab, language and settings move over by themselves, and the panel says “Your searches were kept”. Keep the same folder: Chrome links the extension’s data to it.

Troubleshooting

Stuck? It’s usually one of these.

I don’t see the panel on Grindr Web.

Reload the tab (⌘R / Ctrl+R). Check that the extension’s switch is blue in chrome://extensions and that you’re on web.grindr.com — not the phone app.

Chrome says “Manifest file is missing or unreadable”.

You selected the wrong folder. Pick the folder that directly contains manifest.json (often the one inside the first folder).

I can’t find “Load unpacked”.

Developer mode is off: turn on the switch top-right of chrome://extensions and the button appears top-left.

Chrome shows a warning about developer-mode extensions.

That’s normal for any extension not installed from the store. Click × to dismiss it; it doesn’t affect anything.

Auto-scroll doesn’t move.

Scroll the grid a little by hand first, then press Start again. Grindr sometimes loads slowly.

Grindr Web chat got disconnected after a search.

It can happen after a long, fast scan: Grindr Web may drop its chat connection while thousands of profiles load. We can’t reproduce it ourselves and we don’t control Grindr’s side, so we can’t say for sure what triggers it or promise it’s gone. To get your chat back, reload the Grindr tab (⌘R / Ctrl+R): you stay signed in. For long chats, turn the extension off in chrome://extensions (or use another Chrome profile), or use the Grindr phone app. Since version 2.1.0 the scan is gentler: Scan speed is on Gentle by default, with short breaks taken automatically, while Fast (the old pace) may cause it. To update, download the new .zip and replace the content of your existing folder.

The panel says “Grindr stopped responding”.

Grindr crashed or emptied the grid during a scan, so the scan paused and kept its results in the journal. Reload the Grindr tab if needed, then press Resume. After a reload, an unfinished scan is offered again.

The scan stopped by itself.

It reached the scan time limit (30 minutes by default, set in ⚙). Press Resume to go on, or pick another limit. If the panel warns that Grindr Web is open in another tab, close the other tab: use one tab at a time.
