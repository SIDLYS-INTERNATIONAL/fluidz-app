HOW IT WORKS

Three steps today, one coming later.

1

Start or join a circle

Invite the friends you’d trust with this. Any member can invite too, and everyone sees who invited whom.

2

Check a number

Before you meet, enter his number. See what your friends know — and their friends: each note shows its path, like “2nd circle · via Karim”. Add up to 5 photos to a number; they stay hidden until someone taps to show them.

3

Vouch or flag

Know him? Vouch “safe”. Had a bad time? Flag “be careful” with a short comment. Only your network sees it.

4

Later: check in before the date

Not in the beta yet: tell your circle where you’re headed before you go, so someone always knows where you are.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your circle, your rules.

No public database. No public blacklist. Only people linked by invitations.

Never public

Notes, comments and photos are visible only inside your network. There is no public list of numbers to search.

Any member can invite. The circle admin decides if newcomers are approved by hand or join automatically.

Every note shows its path

Notes come only from people linked to you by invitations, never from the public. You see each one’s degree and path, so you can judge how much it weighs.

A moderated way to report

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is it free?

Yes. Hookup Safety Check is a free Fluidz Labs tool. You just need a free Fluidz ID (one email, no password).

Who can join the beta?

For now it’s invite-only: a friend already in a circle can invite you, or request access here. We can’t promise a date yet.

Who sees what?

There is no public list and no public database. What you post (vouches, flags, comments, photos) is visible only to your network: your circle and the circles linked to it by invitations. Each note shows its degree and path, and photos stay hidden until someone taps to show them.

What happens if a flag is wrong?

A flag is one person’s opinion, not a verdict. It’s visible only inside your network, with its path, so you can weigh it. We’re designing it so you can change what you left, and for serious problems there will be a moderated way to report to Fluidz.

Can someone be removed from a circle?

We’re still deciding how that will work. We’ll explain it clearly before your circle opens.

Does it see my contacts?

It’s built around the numbers you choose to check, not around your address book. The beta will tell you clearly what it needs before you join.

Which apps does it work with?

Any of them. It only needs a number, so it isn’t tied to one app: Grindr, Scruff, Romeo, Hornet, Jack’d, Sniffies or anywhere else you meet people. Fluidz isn’t affiliated with any of them.
