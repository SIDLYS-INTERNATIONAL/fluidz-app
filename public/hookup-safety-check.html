FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is it free?

Yes. Hookup Safety Check is a free Fluidz Labs tool. You just need a free Fluidz ID (one email, no password).

Who can join the beta?

For now it’s invite-only. Request access with your Fluidz ID and we’ll invite people gradually; we can’t promise a date yet.

Does it see my contacts?

It’s built around the numbers and names you choose to check, not around your address book. The beta will tell you clearly what it needs before you join.

Is my number visible to others?

There is no public list of numbers. Information stays inside circles, and you choose who is in yours. The beta will spell out exactly what circle members can see before you join.

What happens if a flag is wrong?

A flag is a friend’s opinion, not a verdict — and it stays inside the circle. We’re designing it so you can change what you left, and for serious problems there will be a moderated way to report to Fluidz.

Which apps does it work with?

Any of them. It only needs a number or a name, so it isn’t tied to one app: Grindr, Scruff, Romeo, Hornet, Jack’d, Sniffies or anywhere else you meet people. Fluidz isn’t affiliated with any of them.
