Who is responsible

fluidz.app and the Profile Name Search extension are published by Fluidz Labs · USA, which decides how your data is used. Contact: hello@fluidz.app.

What we collect when you ask for the download link

The extension is downloaded through a link we send by email. When you enter your email address on fluidz.app, we create your free Fluidz account (no password) and store:

  • Your email address
  • Your language
  • Where you came from, if known (for example the page or the campaign tag in the link you followed)
  • Whether you ticked the box to receive news about the extension and the Fluidz app

Like on any website, our hosting and email providers process technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver pages and messages.

Why we use it

  • To send you the sign-in link and let you download the extension. This is what you asked for.
  • Only if you ticked the box: to tell you about new versions of the extension and the launch of the Fluidz app. This is optional and based on your consent.

The box is not ticked by default, and the download does not depend on it. We don’t use your data for anything else.

Your email is treated as sensitive

An email address on the list of a tool like this can say something about your private life, including your sexual orientation. That is why we treat the list as sensitive: we never sell it, rent it, share it or use it for advertising, and we only send news if you ticked the box. Only the service providers that help us run the site (see below) handle it, on our behalf.

Profile Name Search (the Chrome extension)

The extension runs in your browser and stays out of your data:

  • It reads the profile names shown on the Grindr Web page, inside your browser, so it can match your searches.
  • It keeps your searches, your language and the position of its panel in your browser’s own storage, on your computer.
  • It sends nothing anywhere. Your searches and the names it reads never reach us or anyone else.
  • It never acts on your account: no favorites, no messages, no taps.
  • It doesn’t read your conversations or your messages.
  • It asks Chrome for two permissions, storage and sidePanel, and it only runs on web.grindr.com.
  • Its code is readable, so anyone can check all of this.

Once it’s installed, the extension needs no account and sends nothing. It is independent and not affiliated with Grindr.

Where your data lives

This website is hosted on Vercel. Fluidz accounts are stored with Supabase, in the EU (Ireland). Sign-in links are sent through an email delivery service. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives a normal font request from your browser, including your IP address. Your browser keeps your language choice and, if you’re signed in, your sign-in session, locally on your device, so the site can work.

We keep your data for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it.

What we don’t do

  • No ads.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share your email address.
  • No analytics or advertising scripts on these pages.
  • No tracking cookies.

Your choices and rights

  • News by email is opt-in. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to hello@fluidz.app, and we will stop.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@fluidz.app.
  • If you are in the EU, you can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • Once installed, the extension needs no account.
  • To remove your sign-in session from this browser, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
