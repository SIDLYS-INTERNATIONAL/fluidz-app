What we collect when you create a Fluidz ID

A Fluidz ID is the free account that works across all Fluidz Labs tools. You sign in with a link we email you — no password. You create one, for example, when you unlock the Profile Name Search download or join an early-access list. When you do, we store:

  • Your email address
  • Your language
  • The tool you came from, such as Profile Name Search
  • Campaign tags in the link you followed, if there were any
  • Your marketing choice: whether you want updates by email
  • The tool interests you tick, such as joining an early-access list

We use this to sign you in and to send you what you asked for.

Profile Name Search (the Chrome extension)

The extension runs in your browser and stays out of your data:

  • It reads the profile names shown on the Grindr Web page, inside your browser, so it can match your searches.
  • It keeps your searches, your language and the position of its panel in your browser’s own storage, on your computer.
  • It sends nothing anywhere. Your searches and the names it reads never reach us or anyone else.
  • It never acts on your account: no favorites, no messages, no taps.
  • It asks Chrome for two permissions, storage and sidePanel, and it only runs on web.grindr.com.
  • Its code is readable, so anyone can check all of this.

To download it, you enter your email: that creates your free Fluidz ID and the download unlocks right away. Once it’s installed, the extension needs no account and sends nothing. Details and download on the Profile Name Search page.

Adult Album Creator

The Adult Album Creator is free and needs no account. It works entirely in your browser:

  • Your photos are processed in your browser only. They are never uploaded, not to us and not to anyone else.
  • We never store them, and they aren’t kept on your device either: close or reload the page and they’re gone.
  • GPS and other hidden data are removed, because each photo is re-drawn in your browser before it goes into your images.
  • To save you time, the page keeps your answers — language, tone, captions, what you picked and your rules — in your browser’s local storage, on your device, until you tap “Start over” or switch that off. Your photos are never part of it.
  • The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees a normal font request from your browser (including your IP address). Nothing from your photos or answers is sent with it.
  • No cookies, and no analytics or advertising scripts on this page.

This is general information, not legal advice. Want to try it? Open the Album Creator or see how it works on its own page.

Hookup Safety Check (beta)

It’s an invite-only beta and isn’t open to the public. It will have its own privacy notes when it opens. What members post is visible only inside their network of circles: no public database, no public blacklist.

Where your data lives

This website is hosted on Vercel. Fluidz ID accounts are stored with Supabase, in the EU (Ireland). Sign-in links are sent through an email delivery service. Your browser keeps your language choice and, if you’re signed in, your sign-in session — locally, on your device, so the site can work.

What we don’t do

  • No ads.
  • We don’t sell your email.
  • No analytics or advertising scripts on these pages.
  • No tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Marketing emails are opt-in. You can switch them off any time in My tools.
  • You can sign out any time.
  • Once installed, the extension needs no account.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@fluidz.app.
