FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The album is built inside your browser, on your phone. Not even we can see your photos.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Album Creator is a free Fluidz Labs tool: no watermark, no trial. You just need a free Fluidz ID (one email, no password), so you’re first in line the day it opens.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app where you can keep a private album: Grindr, Scruff, Romeo, Hornet, Jack’d, Sniffies. You upload the finished images yourself, so it isn’t tied to one app.

Why the FLUIDZ stamp?

A small date stamp shows when each photo was taken — proof the pictures are recent, nothing more. It’s on by default and you can turn it off.

Can I write my own captions?

Yes. Captions are suggested from your quiz answers, but every line is editable, and you can shuffle for another version.

Any rules about nudity?

You decide what goes in your album, and each app has its own rules — check them before you upload. The Album Creator is 18+ only, hosts nothing and shares nothing: it just builds images on your phone, with a warning page so nobody sees nudity by surprise.
