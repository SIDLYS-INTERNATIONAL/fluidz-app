How to search Grindr profiles by name (and by emoji)

By Fluidz Labs · Updated 3 November 2026 · 5 min read

You remember he was called Jay — or something with a palm tree next to it. But finding one name in a grid of hundreds of faces means a lot of scrolling and squinting. Here’s how to search the whole grid in a few minutes, by name, keyword or emoji.

Quick answer

Install Profile Name Search, a free Chrome extension. Open web.grindr.com, type a name, a keyword or an emoji in its panel, and start auto-scroll. It pauses on every match, and “Show on grid” takes you straight to him.

Why Ctrl/Cmd+F isn’t enough

Your browser’s Find (Ctrl+F on Windows, Cmd+F on a Mac) only searches text that is already on the page. Grindr Web doesn’t put the whole grid on the page at once: it loads profiles in batches as you scroll and, like most long lists, keeps only the ones near your screen. This is called a virtual list.

So Find only ever sees a few dozen profiles. The guy you want may be hundreds of places further down, and as far as Find is concerned he isn’t there yet.

Step by step with Profile Name Search

  1. Install the extension

    Download it free from the download page and follow the 2-minute install. It works in Chrome 116+ and in Brave, Edge and Arc — not Safari or Firefox.

  2. Open Grindr Web

    Go to web.grindr.com and sign in as usual, then open the extension from your toolbar. The best way is Chrome’s side panel: it sits next to the page and never covers the grid.

  3. Type what you’re looking for

    Enter a name, a keyword or an emoji. It searches the name shown on each profile card, and upper or lower case doesn’t matter. Combine terms like this:

    You typeWhat it finds
    JayA nameEvery profile whose name contains “jay”: Jay, Jayden, jay_bcn…
    dom topA keywordMany guys put tags in their name: DOM TOP, vers, bear… Search them like a name.
    🚀An emoji🚀, 🌴, 😈… emojis in names work too.
    jay, mikeOR — commaEither one: profiles named Jay or Mike, in one pass.
    dom + topAND — plusBoth words, any order: “DOM TOP 🚀” and “top dom” match, “dom” alone doesn’t.
    "dom top"Exact phraseOnly these words, in this order.

  4. Start auto-scroll

    Press “Start auto-scroll” and let it run: it scrolls the grid for you and loads new profiles. Keep “Pause at each new match” on and it stops every time it finds one.

  5. Jump to him with “Show on grid”

    Click a match in the list, then “Show on grid”: the page scrolls back to that profile, even if you’ve already scrolled past it. Press Resume to carry on down the grid.

Tips

  • Save a search. You can keep up to 3 saved searches — handy for the names you look for again and again.
  • Use the favorites filter. “Only my favorites” narrows the results to guys you’ve already favorited.
  • Keep it in the side panel. Toolbar icon → “Open in Chrome’s side panel”, and the grid stays fully visible.
  • Search tags too. Many guys put tags in their name — DOM TOP, vers, bear — so keywords work just like names.

What it can’t do

  • It only sees what Grindr shows you. The limit is your Grindr plan, not the extension: when auto-scroll stops, you’ve seen every profile your plan shows.
  • It never acts for you. No clicks, no messages, no favorites. It reads the names on your screen and scrolls the grid, nothing else.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. Your searches stay in your browser’s storage and the extension sends nothing anywhere. More on our privacy page.
  • It’s desktop only. It runs on web.grindr.com in a Chrome-type browser, not in the phone app.
Grindr’s terms are strict about third-party tools. The extension never acts on your account, but auto-scroll is a grey zone, so use it at your own discretion.

Quick questions

Can I search by emoji?

Yes. Emojis in names work like any other text: try 🚀 or 🌴, or mix them with words, like jay, 🌴.

Does it work in the Grindr phone app?

No. It’s a Chrome extension, so it only runs on web.grindr.com in a desktop browser. On your phone, leave your email on the download page and we’ll send you a link to open on your computer.

Will it find guys who aren’t on my grid?

No. It can only match the profiles Grindr shows you, up to the limit of your Grindr plan. If he isn’t in your grid, he won’t be in the results.
